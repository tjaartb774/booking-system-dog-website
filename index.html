Our approach

Different space. Same hands. Same heart.

Tap or hover a card to see what each promise means for your dog.

Force-Free

We never prioritise finishing a groom over your dog's wellbeing.

No forcing. Ever.

If your dog becomes frightened or exhausted, we give breaks, adapt the groom, or stop. You only pay for the work reasonably completed and the time spent caring for your dog.

Fun

Grooming should become something your dog can enjoy.

Treats, breaks, play

Early, gentle introductions and shorter, more frequent sessions help dogs learn that the grooming table is a safe, even happy, place to be.

Trust

Our aim is to build trust, not force compliance.

Patience before speed

Kindness, trust and safety come first. Senior dogs and dogs with medical conditions get extra consideration for their comfort and limits.

Services
