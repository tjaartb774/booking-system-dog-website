Booking Policy & Terms

At Beyond the Leash, every dog is treated as an individual. Our priority is to provide a safe, positive and compassionate grooming experience while maintaining the wellbeing of the dogs in our care. By making a booking with Beyond the Leash, you acknowledge and agree to the following.

Force-Free•Fun•Trust

Appointments & Arrival▾

Please arrive at your scheduled appointment time.

We understand that life happens, but arriving late may affect the time available for your dog's groom. If you are significantly late, we may need to adjust the service or reschedule the appointment to avoid affecting other clients.

Please let us know as soon as possible if you are running late.

Cancellations & No-Shows▾

Our appointment times are reserved specifically for your dog.

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give us at least 24 hours' notice wherever possible.

Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may result in a booking deposit being required before future appointments are confirmed.

Clients who repeatedly fail to arrive for appointments without contacting us may be required to pay for the missed appointment before another booking is accepted.

We completely understand that genuine emergencies happen and will always consider these individually.

Force-Free Grooming▾

Beyond the Leash follows a force-free, compassionate approach to grooming.

We will never prioritise completing a groom over your dog's physical or emotional wellbeing.

If your dog becomes excessively frightened, distressed, exhausted, aggressive or unable to safely tolerate part of the grooming process, we may:

  • Give your dog additional breaks
  • Modify the groom
  • Stop a particular procedure
  • End the grooming session early
  • Recommend shorter or more frequent grooming sessions
  • Suggest veterinary or behavioural assistance where appropriate

You will only be charged for the work reasonably completed and/or the time spent caring for and working with your dog.

Our aim is to build trust, not force compliance.

Matted Coats▾

Severe matting can be painful and may hide skin irritation, sores, parasites or other conditions.

Where brushing or dematting would cause unnecessary discomfort, we may recommend clipping the coat shorter.

Your dog's comfort will always take priority over maintaining a particular hairstyle.

Removal of severe matting can occasionally reveal existing skin conditions or cause temporary redness or sensitivity. We will take reasonable care to minimise discomfort and will inform you of anything concerning that we discover.

Additional charges may apply for significantly matted coats because of the extra time, equipment wear and care required. Wherever practical, this will be discussed with you.

Behaviour▾

Please tell us before the appointment if your dog:

  • Has previously bitten or attempted to bite
  • Is nervous or reactive
  • Dislikes particular areas being handled
  • Has had a difficult grooming experience
  • Has known triggers
  • Requires special handling

This does not automatically mean we will refuse your dog.

Knowing this information helps us prepare properly and make the experience safer and more comfortable for everyone.

However, Beyond the Leash reserves the right to stop a groom if continuing would create an unreasonable risk to the dog or groomer.

Health & Medical Conditions▾

Please inform us of any medical conditions, injuries, allergies, skin problems, seizures, heart conditions, recent operations or other health concerns before grooming begins.

Senior dogs and dogs with medical conditions will be groomed with additional consideration for their comfort and limitations.

If we discover something concerning during grooming, we will bring it to your attention. Groomers cannot diagnose medical conditions, and veterinary advice may be recommended.

In the event of a genuine medical emergency while your dog is in our care, we will attempt to contact you immediately. If you cannot be reached and urgent veterinary attention is reasonably necessary for your dog's welfare, you authorise us to seek veterinary assistance.

Veterinary costs remain the responsibility of the dog's owner unless Beyond the Leash is legally responsible for the injury or incident.

Fleas, Ticks & Contagious Conditions▾

Please do not bring your dog to the parlour if they have a known contagious illness or condition.

If fleas or ticks are discovered during grooming, appropriate action may be taken to protect your dog and the other dogs using our premises.

Additional charges may apply where additional treatment, cleaning or disinfection is required.

If your dog is unwell before an appointment, please contact us so that we can decide together whether the appointment should be postponed.

Puppies & Vaccinations▾

For the safety of your puppy and other dogs, puppies should have received the vaccinations appropriate for their age before attending the parlour.

Please discuss your puppy's vaccination status with us when booking their first visit.

Early, gentle grooming introductions are encouraged and form an important part of building a positive relationship with grooming.

Collection▾

We will contact you when your dog is ready for collection.

Because we aim to maintain a calm environment and do not operate as a doggy daycare facility, please collect your dog within a reasonable period after being notified.

If you are going to be delayed, please contact us.

Repeated significantly late collections may result in an additional care fee.

Grooming Results▾

We will always try to achieve the style requested, but the final result depends on factors including:

  • Coat type and condition
  • Matting
  • Previous grooming
  • Your dog's behaviour and tolerance
  • Skin or medical conditions
  • The amount of coat available to work with

Where the requested style cannot safely or comfortably be achieved, your dog's wellbeing will come first.

Photos & Social Media▾

We love celebrating our Beyond the Leash floofs!

With your permission, photographs or short videos of your dog may be taken during or after their visit and used on Beyond the Leash social media, website or marketing materials.

You are completely welcome to decline photography. This will never affect the care your dog receives.

Personal Belongings▾

Please ensure collars, harnesses and leads are in safe working condition.

Beyond the Leash cannot accept responsibility for ordinary wear, damage or loss of personal accessories unless caused by our negligence.

Pricing▾

Prices are based on factors including breed, size, coat type, coat condition, behaviour, grooming requirements and the time required to complete the service.

Quoted prices may therefore be estimates rather than a guaranteed final price.

Additional charges may apply for circumstances such as severe matting, excessive coat, fleas, additional handling requirements or significantly extended grooming time.

Where reasonably possible, we will discuss substantial additional charges with you before proceeding.

Client Agreement▾

By confirming an appointment with Beyond the Leash, I confirm that:

  • I am the owner of the dog or have authority to make decisions regarding the dog's care.
  • I have provided accurate information regarding my dog's health, behaviour and grooming history to the best of my knowledge.
  • I understand and accept Beyond the Leash's force-free grooming approach.
  • I understand that a groom may be changed or stopped where continuing would compromise the dog's welfare or safety.
  • I have read and agree to the Beyond the Leash Booking Policy & Terms.
Our promise

The dog in front of us matters more than finishing the groom.

Beyond the Leash is built around that one simple belief. We want grooming to become something your dog can understand, tolerate and hopefully even enjoy.

Patience, kindness, trust and safety will always come before speed.
